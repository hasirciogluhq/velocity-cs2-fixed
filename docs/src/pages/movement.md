| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bunny hop | Jumps again the moment you land. | On |
| Air strafe | Keeps your speed in the air, in every direction. | On |
| Jump bug | Lands a jump without losing speed off a ledge. | On |
| Fast ladder | Climbs ladders at full speed. | On |
| Edge jump | Jumps at the last moment before a ledge. | Hold E |
| Edge stop | Stops you right at the edge. | Hold N |
| Edge bug | Lands on an edge without fall damage, with several modes. | Off |
| Slow walk | Walks at a speed you set. | Hold P |

A speed readout on the HUD shows your velocity and its peak, reset on landing if you like.
