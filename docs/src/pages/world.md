## Weather

Snow, rain or a night of stars, with fog, wind and wet surfaces you can tune: density, draw distance, wind strength and direction.

## Sky and light

| Option | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Skybox | Swap the sky for another one, or tint it. |
| Sun and clouds | Their colours. |
| Lighting | The colour, strength and angle of the map's light. |
| World colour | A tint over walls and floors. |
| Ambient | The light that fills the shadows. |

## Post-processing

Bloom, gamma, depth of field and chromatic aberration, each with its own strength. A dynamic light can follow you around the map.

## Removals

Hide smoke, the scope overlay, recoil shake, decals, legs, the 3D skybox, fog or overhead icons, one by one.
