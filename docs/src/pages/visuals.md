## Players

| Option | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Box | A frame around each player. |
| Skeleton | The player's bones, drawn as lines. |
| Health and ammo bars | Bars beside the box, coloured by how much is left. |
| Name and weapon | Who it is and what they hold. |
| Flags | Money, armour, kit, scoped, flashed and defusing, each in its own colour. |
| Off-screen arrows | Arrows at the edge of the screen for players behind you. |

## Chams and glow

Recolour players, your own arms and weapon, and dropped items. Chams stack in layers, so a solid colour and an overlay can sit on the same model. Glow outlines whatever you pick.

## Items and grenades

- Dropped pistols, rifles, SMGs, shotguns, snipers and utility, with names or icons and a distance limit.
- Grenade trajectories before you throw.
- Molotov and incendiary fires drawn as filled, outlined areas.
- Tracers that follow grenades in flight.

## Hits

Hit markers, hit and death sounds, bullet tracers and impacts, and a log of hits and misses in the console or chat.
