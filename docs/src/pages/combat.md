## Legitbot

Aim help that stays close to how a person plays.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Aimbot | Pulls your aim toward a target inside a field of view you set. |
| Recoil control | Counters spray patterns, with or without the aimbot. |
| Triggerbot | Fires when your crosshair rests on a target, optionally head only. |
| Autowall | Considers targets behind thin walls. |
| Visualize fov | Draws the aim circle on screen while you tune it. |

## Ragebot

The full set, for practice servers and bot matches.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hit chance and minimum damage | Wait for a shot worth taking, with keybind overrides. |
| Point scale | Choose how far into each hitbox it aims, or let it adapt. |
| Autostop | Stops you for an accurate shot. |
| Force body aim | Switches to the body on a key. |
| Extrapolation | Leads moving targets. |

## Per weapon

Both modes keep separate settings for each weapon group, so a pistol and a sniper never share a profile.

## Extras

Quick peek, duck peek, a Zeus bot, a knife bot, auto revolver, auto scope and a penetration crosshair that shows whether a wall can be shot through.
