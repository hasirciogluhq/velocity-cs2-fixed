## What you can change

| Item | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Guns | Every finish in the game, with wear, seed and StatTrak. |
| Knives | Any knife model with any knife finish. |
| Gloves | Any glove model and finish. |
| Agents | The agent you play as, on either side. |

## How it works in play

Open the skin browser, pick a finish from the grid and set its wear, seed and StatTrak. Your choices are saved with your config.

Skins are visible to you only. Other players see your default items.
