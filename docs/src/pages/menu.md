## Tabs

Ragebot, Legitbot, Players, World, Skins, Misc and Config. Every option sits under the tab you would look in first.

## Search

Type part of a name, such as "fog" or "glow", and every matching option appears in one list, wherever it lives.

## Keybinds

Any option with a switch can take a key, in one of three modes:

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Toggle | Press once for on, again for off. |
| Hold on | On while the key is held. |
| Hold off | Off while the key is held. |

## Configs

Save as many configs as you like, load one in a click, and keep a separate one for each way you play. Skins, colours and binds are saved with it.

## HUD

A custom crosshair and scope, a velocity readout, a watermark and glass widgets for fps, ping, time and speed, with your Steam avatar. Every colour is yours to pick.
